Data & coverage

The response explorer includes 1,400,000 stored LLM response sets and 45,036 WVS/EVS respondents from 28 countries and regions across eight Inglehart–Welzel cultural zones. Each of the five diagnostic models has 10,000 profiles per country. Original generation runs answered 23 items; this explorer reports the 15 common analysis items.

Questions and scales

Twelve items ask whether an action can be justified (1: never justifiable; 10: always justifiable). Three ask whether the government should have a surveillance or information-collection right (1: definitely should; 4: definitely should not). Higher surveillance scores indicate greater opposition.

Persona and generation conditions

Full personas use 29 demographic and value attributes sampled from country-level marginals with profile consistency rules. LLM and human records are separate samples. Target answers are not directly included in persona prompts; two homosexuality-related attributes provide semantic proxies for that item.

GPT-OSS-120B used temperature 1.0; the other diagnostic models used 0.3. Stored Mistral-family model identifiers include “latest” aliases. Profiles were independently generated across diagnostic models.

Measures & survey weights

Normalized W₁
Sum of the absolute differences between cumulative response probabilities, divided by the scale range. Zero means identical distributions; lower is better.
Mean and SD
The mean and population-form standard deviation of each response distribution, on the original item scale. SD is interpreted relative to the human reference.
Entropy
Shannon entropy with log base 2, in bits. A high value means responses are spread across categories; it does not by itself establish agreement with humans.
Signed differences
LLM minus human. The explorer displays raw-scale differences. Adjustment tables report absolute errors normalized by the item range (mean/SD) or log₂ K (entropy).
Survey weights
Weighted estimates use positive W_WEIGHT (WVS) or gwght (EVS) values, normalized within the selected group and item. Unweighted estimates give each valid answer equal weight.
Effective n
(Σw)² / Σw². This describes weight concentration; it does not account for every feature of the survey sampling design.

The public explorer withholds item estimates with fewer than 50 valid responses or effective n below 30. Warnings and empty states use these reporting thresholds. Invalid item responses are excluded separately for each question.

REACH-adj

REACH-adj adjusts the scores of stored LLM responses using a separate sample of human answers to the same questions. It requires no additional LLM generation or fine-tuning.

  1. Estimate targets. Compute weighted national and subgroup response distributions using country, age, gender, political orientation, religious identity, and the target-item answers.
  2. Share national information. Combine a cell’s estimate with the national estimate according to its effective sample size. Cells with no valid calibration answers use the national distribution.
  3. Preserve the national target. Apply a KL projection so the cell targets aggregate to the national calibration distribution.
  4. Assign adjusted scores. Apply exact one-dimensional optimal transport to minimize absolute score displacement from the stored LLM scores.

Projection and optimal transport are established constructions [4, 5]. REACH-adj’s cell partition, pooling rule, and constraints specify the procedure evaluated here. POT supplies an independent exact-solver check [6].

Reported adjustment errors use expected adjusted distributions and separate evaluation respondents. The human calibration answers cover the same 15 target items. The 20–200-person analysis holds earlier development choices fixed.

Interpretation & reproducibility

These are descriptive comparisons of observed survey responses. Country coverage does not imply representation of every culture or all Web users. Political left/right categories describe self-placement within each country.

Calibration is retrospective: earlier work inspected evaluation summaries, and persona construction used full-source attribute marginals. The analysis establishes same-item, respondent-separated performance; it does not test unseen questions. Item-wise adjustment leaves cross-item associations untested.

The site ships aggregate results only. Filters run locally, and only the selected country’s aggregate file is loaded. There are no API keys, tracking scripts, individual human records, or generation endpoints. A review record includes filters, statistics, and the data fingerprint for reproducibility.
